## 3. Global Markets QIS Collaboration with BNPP AM: Launch of 4 Systematic ETFs
* **Date:** July 16, 2026
* **Sources & Links:**
  * [RankiaPro – BNP Paribas AM launches 4 new generation active ETFs](https://rankiapro.com/en/news/bnp-paribas-am-launches-4-new-generation-active-etfs/)
* **Summary:**
  * BNP Paribas Asset Management launched four actively managed ETFs under the *BNP Paribas Easy* umbrella:
    1. **BNP Paribas Easy Equity Premium Income:** Systematic options premium harvesting on major equity indices.
    2. **BNP Paribas Easy European Equity Buffer:** Structured outcome-oriented defined-risk protection on the Euro Stoxx 50.
    3. **BNP Paribas Easy Global Equity Long Short:** Synthetic factor-driven long/short alpha strategy using MSCI Barra multifactor models.
    4. **BNP Paribas Easy Managed Futures:** Systematic CTA trend-following allocation across multi-asset futures and forwards.
  * **Team Collaboration:** The launch was built on an alliance between **BNP Paribas Asset Management** and the **Global Markets division of BNP Paribas**, combining Global Markets' proprietary quantitative investment strategies, internal systematic models, and synthetic structuring capabilities with BNPP AM's ETF and portfolio infrastructure.
