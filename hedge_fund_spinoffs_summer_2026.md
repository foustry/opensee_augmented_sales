## Executive Summary

During the summer of 2026, the alternative investment industry experienced a notable resurgence in hedge fund formations, spin-offs, and multi-manager external allocations. Key drivers include:
- **War for External Talent:** Major multistrats (Millennium, Schonfeld, Squarepoint, Walleye, Verition) increasingly allocated external capital via Separately Managed Accounts (SMAs) to top-tier portfolio managers spinning out of **Citadel**.
- **Quant & Asia Expansion:** Marquee quantitative launches anchored by institutional giants (e.g., Blackstone committing $300M to Two Sigma veteran Jinger Zhao's **z Star Research**; Millennium backing former Citadel quant Paul Dou's **Third Epsilon** in Hong Kong).
- **Mega-Launches:** Silicon Valley tech equity spin-offs such as Jeff Wang's **Augnition Global Investors** targeting $2B+ at debut.
- **Macro Backdrop:** HFR data revealed 166 new hedge funds launched in early 2026, pushing global hedge fund industry assets to a record **$5.22 trillion**.

## 1. 🏛️ Citadel Spin-Offs & Alumni Hedge Fund Launches

*Source: [Business Insider – July 27, 2026](https://www.businessinsider.com/citadel-spin-offs-favorite-for-hedge-funds-investing-externally-2026-7)*

A comprehensive industry analysis revealed that institutional multistrategy funds are heavily deploying external capital into at least **12 funds run by former Citadel portfolio managers**:

### A. Third Epsilon (Hong Kong)
* **Founder:** **Paul Dou** (former Citadel quantitative portfolio manager who departed in 2024).
* **Strategy:** Systematic and quantitative market-neutral equity focused on Asia-Pacific markets.
* **Anchor / Backer:** Backed by Izzy Englander's **Millennium Management**.

### B. FIFTHDELTA (London)
* **Founders:** **Niall O'Keefe** and **Tio Charbaghi** (former Citadel portfolio managers).
* **Strategy:** Fundamental market-neutral equity long/short.
* **Backers:** Secured external SMA allocations from major multi-manager platforms including **Squarepoint Capital** and **Walleye Capital**, alongside original commingled fund investors.

### C. Meridiem Capital
* **Founder:** **Khalid Malik** (former Citadel portfolio manager).
* **Backers:** Backed by **Schonfeld Strategic Advisors** (after Millennium previously redeemed).

### D. Kodai Capital
* **Founder:** **Neville Shah** (onetime Citadel portfolio manager).
* **Status:** Managed external SMA capital for Millennium before returning investor capital; Shah transitioned into an internal portfolio manager role at Millennium.

### E. Citadel's External Alpha Capture Program
* *Source: [LinkedIn / Bloomberg – June 3, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/citadel-expands-quant-platform-new-hedge-fund-signal-u4sgf)*
* **Details:** Ken Griffin’s Citadel expanded its **Global Quantitative Strategies (GQS)** unit to establish an external signal-sharing program, compensating emerging hedge funds and discretionary portfolio managers for proprietary trading signals fed into Citadel’s systematic models.
