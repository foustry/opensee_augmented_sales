## 🎯 Executive Summary & Opensee Value Proposition Fit

Opensee provides real-time analytics, risk management, and market data solutions capable of processing billions of trade ticks without data aggregation or truncation. For a **Trade Analytics Webinar**, the ideal attendees fall into three core personas:

1. **Heads of Execution & Electronic Trading Desks (Buy-Side & Sell-Side):**
   * *Pain Points:* High-frequency tick data processing, transaction cost analysis (TCA), multi-venue Best Execution compliance, algo slippage measurement, and smart order routing benchmarking.
2. **Heads of Trading, Capital Markets & FICC / Derivatives:**
   * *Pain Points:* Real-time intraday P&L attribution, intraday Greeks calculation, position tracking, and post-trade analytics.
3. **Chief Risk Officers (CRO) & Heads of Quantitative Risk / Market Risk:**
   * *Pain Points:* Real-time stress testing, intraday Value-at-Risk (VaR), limit breach alerts, and unified trade surveillance.

## 💡 Suggested Webinar Narrative & Tracks

1. **Track 1: "Taming the Tick: Real-Time Execution Quality & Multi-Asset TCA"**
   * *Target Attendees:* Heads of Execution & Electronic Trading Desks (Marc Both, Martin Hendry, Nicolas Bader, Lars Salmon, Christopher Limentani).
   * *Key Topics:* Granular TCA on high-volume electronic flow, smart order routing performance, slippage vs. arrival price, and eliminating data aggregation latency.

2. **Track 2: "Real-Time Greeks, Intraday P&L Attribution & Risk on Massive Derivatives Flows"**
   * *Target Attendees:* Heads of Capital Markets & Quant Trading Desks (Hatem Mustapha, Kavi Gupta, Mike DiSpirito, Chris Drew).
   * *Key Topics:* Real-time intraday risk calculation, P&L explain across volatile market sessions, and handling complex derivatives structures at scale.

3. **Track 3: "Consolidating Trade Surveillance, Best Execution & Risk on a Single Lakehouse"**
   * *Target Attendees:* CROs, Heads of Market Risk, and Heads of Surveillance Tech (Boudewijn Duinstra, Dheeraj Soni, Marcus Chromik, Puneet Pardasani).
   * *Key Topics:* Breaking down data silos between front-office execution desks and middle-office risk/compliance, lowering TCO while ensuring full auditability of all trade events.
