## 📌 Desk: Model Risk
### [Beyond mirror validation: the cost of ineffective challenge in model risk management](https://www.risk.net/journal-of-risk-model-validation/7964188/beyond-mirror-validation-the-cost-of-ineffective-challenge-in-model-risk-management)
- **Published Date:** September 27, 2026
- **Authors / Desk:** Gustavo Coelho Haase, Paulo Henrique Dourado, João Paulo Vieira Costa, Eduardo Medeiros Rubik
- **Article Link:** [`https://www.risk.net/journal-of-risk-model-validation/7964188/beyond-mirror-validation-the-cost-of-ineffective-challenge-in-model-risk-management`](https://www.risk.net/journal-of-risk-model-validation/7964188/beyond-mirror-validation-the-cost-of-ineffective-challenge-in-model-risk-management)
- **Tagged Entities:** Banco do Brasil, US Federal Reserve (SR 11-7, SR 26-2), Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC)

**Executive Summary:**
Demonstrates the severe pitfalls of 'mirror validation'—where validators merely re-execute model logic and check discrimination metrics (Gini, KS, AUC) without stress-testing calibration under regime changes. Across 500 simulations of retail credit PD models, mirror validation missed an average 32% underestimation of default probability, triggering an 8.5% loan-level capital shortfall. Presents an adversarial 5-test framework implementing supervisory 'effective challenge' requirements.
